Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Overview

Methoryn is a local-first command-line application. It runs on your machine and communicates directly with third-party AI and Google services using API keys that you provide. We do not operate servers, collect analytics, or store your data.

Data We Do Not Collect

Methoryn does not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your prompts or conversations
  • Your API keys
  • Files you read or write through the CLI
  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Usage statistics or telemetry of any kind

API Keys

API keys for Groq, Gemini, and other services are stored locally on your machine in a .env file or your system keystore. They are sent directly from your machine to the respective provider (Groq, Google) and are never transmitted to us.

Google OAuth and Google Services

Methoryn uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access Google services on your behalf (Gmail, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides). When you sign in:

  • Your OAuth token is stored locally on your machine only.
  • Methoryn accesses your Google data solely to execute the actions you explicitly request in the CLI.
  • We do not read, store, or transmit your Google account data to any third party other than Google itself.
  • You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Methoryn's use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-Party Services

When you send a prompt, your input is sent to the AI provider you have configured (Groq or Google Gemini). Your use of those services is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Groq Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Local Storage

Methoryn stores the following data locally on your machine only:

  • Your API keys (in .env or byok.json)
  • Your Google OAuth token
  • Session history files (optional, in the sessions/ folder)
  • Any files you explicitly ask Methoryn to create or save

None of this data leaves your machine except as part of API requests you initiate.

Children's Privacy

Methoryn is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Continued use of Methoryn after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
